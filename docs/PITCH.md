# Pitch outline (3–5 min, adjust to the official format)

1. **Problem** (15 s) — who, what pain, one concrete example
2. **Solution** (15 s) — what we built, one sentence
3. **Live demo** (45 s) — one happy path, pre-tested, with a backup video
4. **Why Apertus / why open & sovereign** (30 s)
5. **Team** (15 s)

## Demo checklist
- [ ] Demo input prepared and tested
- [ ] Backup screen recording
- [ ] Wi-Fi-independent fallback
- [ ] Who speaks, who clicks
